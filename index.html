How to read this

  • Add data tab — enter a snapshot by hand (date/time + a row per holding with quantity, amount, and optional return %). Each snapshot you add can be edited or deleted from the list. It all lives in this browser’s localStorage only — use Export JSON to keep a copy, Import JSON to restore it or move it to another device.
  • Time series tab plots any combination of the three totals and the individual holdings. Euros fits the axis to your selection; Indexed rebases each line to 100. The hover tooltip shows the share count held at that date next to each holding.
  • Share counts over time: the table below has an Amount € / Shares held switch — “Shares held” shows the quantity of each holding at every snapshot (last column = number of positions). On All stocks, scrubbing a panel updates its share count too.
  • Base data: 74 snapshots, 10 Jun 2023 – 31 Aug 2026, dated by EXIF. Very unevenly spaced — clusters of near-daily shots, then multi-month gaps (nothing between Jul 2023 and Mar 2024, or Jul–Sep 2025). Every screenshot that showed an “Ações” total reconciles to the cent against the summed rows.
  • The book grew from 5 holdings to 13. Started as Tesla + VW + Lufthansa + Meta + the S&P 500 ETF; Palantir, Nvidia and ASML were added end-Jan 2025; Oracle around Nov 2025; AMD and Qualcomm by Feb 2026; ServiceNow in May 2026; Microsoft in Aug 2026. Share counts also stepped up (Tesla 208→228→235, VW 7→14, Oracle 5→13, Qualcomm 15→23, Palantir 15→20, ServiceNow 10→18). A jump in a line at a ◯ is partly a purchase, not price — so the multi-year “since” return is inflated by a lot of added capital.
  • Rent % (the app’s return-vs-cost column) was captured for the 2023 – May 2026 screenshots and the final one, but not for the mid-May – 22 Aug 2026 batch — so the unrealised-return estimate uses the most recent snapshot that carries it.
  • The S&P 500 ETF (SPDR / State Street, 5 units held throughout, ~€2.0k in 2023 rising to ~€3.3k) is now included as the 13th holding. A few of its values (2025 gap, mid-2026) were interpolated where the ETF card was scrolled off the screenshot.
