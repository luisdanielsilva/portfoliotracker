Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

Portfolio Tracker is a personal project run by Luís Silva. It exists to track a stock portfolio over time. This page describes exactly what it stores, who else sees it, and how to have it removed. It is deliberately short, because the app does comparatively little with your data.

What is stored

DataWhy
Your email addressIt is your account identity. Sign-in works by emailing you a link, or through Google.
Transactions you enterTicker, quantity, amount, currency, exchange rate and date. Everything the app shows is calculated from these.
Alert rules you createTo know when to email you, and when it last did so.
A session cookieTo keep you signed in. One cookie, pt_session, HttpOnly, valid 30 days.
Contact form messagesThe name, email and message you submit, so a reply can be sent.

What is not stored

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no third-party cookies. The site loads no tracking scripts of any kind.
  • No passwords — there are none to store.
  • No payment details. The app is free and takes no payments.
  • Your data is never sold, rented, or shared for marketing.

Who else sees it

ServiceWhat it receives
Hetzner (hosting, EU)The server the app and its database run on.
Resend (email delivery)Your email address and the contents of messages sent to you — login links and alert emails.
Google (sign-in)Only if you choose "Continue with Google". Google confirms your identity; the app receives your email address. The app never sees your Google password.
Yahoo Finance (prices)Ticker symbols only. No personal data is sent — it is asked what a share costs, not who is asking.
Google FontsYour browser fetches the page's fonts from Google's servers, which means Google can see your IP address when you load the page.

How it is protected

  • All traffic is over HTTPS.
  • The session cookie is HttpOnly, so page scripts cannot read it.
  • Session and login tokens are stored hashed. Someone who obtained a copy of the database could not use it to sign in as you.
  • Login links are single-use and expire after 15 minutes.

How long it is kept

Your account and transactions are kept until you ask for them to be deleted. Login links expire after 15 minutes and sessions after 30 days. Database backups are kept for 30 days and then deleted, so data can persist in a backup for up to a month after you delete it.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or have your account and everything in it permanently deleted. Email luisdanielsilva@gmail.com and it will be done. You do not need to give a reason.

If you are in the EU or UK, the GDPR gives you these rights by law, along with the right to complain to your national data protection authority. The lawful basis for storing your email address is performing the service you asked for; for everything else, it is the data you chose to enter.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who receives it, the date at the top changes and signed-in users are emailed.
